# Sales_Databrics_Dashboard

This Sales Transaction Dashboard provides an interactive overview of sales performance using transaction data. It helps users understand product-wise sales, sales trends, quantity sold, and payment method distribution.

Key Features
Product-wise Sales Analysis – Compares total sales by product and identifies the highest-selling products.
Quantity Sold Over Time – Shows how the number of products sold changes across different dates.
Sales Trend Analysis – Tracks daily sales performance and highlights increases and decreases over time.
Payment Method Breakdown – Displays the percentage contribution of different payment methods such as American Express, Mastercard, and Visa.
Interactive Quantity Filter – Users can filter transactions based on quantity to analyze specific transaction ranges.
Business Insights

The dashboard can help stakeholders:

Identify top-performing products
Monitor daily sales trends
Understand customer payment preferences
Detect periods of high and low sales activity
Make better sales and business decisions
